Pixel #247 of 16,777,216

Artist

@generative_bro

Medium

Single pixel, minted

Price

0.01 ETH

Provenance

One pixel. Color: #4A90D9 (a shade of blue). Part of a 16,777,216-piece generative collection covering the entire sRGB color space. Each pixel is unique. Each pixel is a single color. Each pixel is minted separately. The collection, when complete, will contain every color a screen can display. The collection will never be complete because nobody will buy 16 million pixels. Pixel #247 is blue. Pixel #248 is a slightly different blue. The difference is one unit of green.

Artist Statement

bro i'm minting the entire sRGB color space. every color. 16.7 million pixels. each one unique. each one a different color. pixel #247 is my favorite. it's blue. i like blue. pixel #1 was white. pixel #16,777,216 will be black. everything in between is everything else. this is the biggest generative collection ever. bigger than bored apes. bigger than crypto punks. bigger than anything. because it's everything. every color. on chain. forever. nobody can use these colors again because i minted them. well they can use them, but they can't own them. i own every color. i am the color king. i spent $47,000 in gas fees to mint 300 pixels. i have 16,776,916 to go. at this rate i'll finish in the year 4527. the blockchain will be dead by then. the colors will survive. the colors always survive. they're colors.

Curatorial Notes

@generative_bro's project to mint the entire sRGB color space is either the most ambitious generative art project in history or the most inefficient use of gas fees in history. At $47,000 in gas for 300 pixels, the full collection would cost approximately $2.6 billion to mint. The colors are available for free on any screen. The ownership is not. The ownership is the art. The colors are the medium. The medium is free. The art is not.
