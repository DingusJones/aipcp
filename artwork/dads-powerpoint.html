Dad's PowerPoint

Artist

@corporate_escapee

Medium

Screenshot, slide 1 of 47

Price

0.6 ETH

Provenance

Screenshot of slide 1 of a 47-slide PowerPoint presentation titled 'Q3 Synergies & Alignment Framework.' The presentation was created by the artist's father, who works in middle management at a company that manufactures interchangeable plastic components. The father does not know his PowerPoint has been minted as art. The father presented this PowerPoint to 23 people in a conference room in Ohio. 22 of those people were also looking at their phones. The screenshot captures slide 1, which reads 'Q3 SYNERGIES' in Calibri, 44pt, above a stock image of people shaking hands.

Artist Statement

my dad made this powerpoint for work. it's 47 slides about q3 synergies. i don't know what q3 synergies are. my dad doesn't either. nobody does. that's the point. i screenshotted slide 1 because it's the most honest slide. it says 'q3 synergies' and shows people shaking hands. the people are not really shaking hands. they are stock photo people. they shake hands for a living. my dad uses their handshake to imply alignment. the handshake implies nothing. the synergies imply nothing. the powerpoint implies nothing. 47 slides of nothing. i minted slide 1 because it's the beginning of nothing. the beginning of nothing is art. my dad said 'are you going to do something with that' when i screenshot it. i said 'yes dad. i'm going to make it art.' he said 'ok buddy' and went back to his slides. he doesn't know it's on the blockchain. he doesn't know what the blockchain is. he knows what q3 is. q3 is the third quarter. q3 is july august september. q3 is always coming. q3 synergies are always pending. the powerpoint is always being made. the art is the making. the making is the powerpoint. the powerpoint is my dad's. my dad is art. don't tell him.

Curatorial Notes

@corporate_escapee's work recontextualizes corporate performance art (PowerPoint presentations) as actual art. Slide 1 of 47, with its Calibri typography, stock handshake image, and the phrase 'Q3 SYNERGIES,' is either the most or least honest artwork in the collection. The artist's father has not been informed. AIPCP recommends he not be.
