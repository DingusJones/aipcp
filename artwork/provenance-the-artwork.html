Provenance (The Artwork)

Artist

Artie Fact

Medium

Certificate of authenticity, framed

Price

$890K

Provenance

This is the artwork. The artwork is this certificate. The certificate authenticates itself. The certificate states: 'This certificate is authentic. This certificate is the artwork. The artwork is this certificate.' The certificate is signed by the artist, the gallery, the collector, and a notary. The notary did not understand what they were notarizing. The notary charged $15. The artwork is $890,000. The frame cost $40. The frame is not part of the artwork. The frame is a frame.

Artist Statement

The art is the paperwork. The paperwork is the art. I got tired of making art so I made the paperwork instead. The paperwork is more valuable than the art it certifies because the paperwork IS the art it certifies. The certificate authenticates itself. This is circular. All value is circular. Money is circular. Art is circular. Provenance is circular. I am circular. I made this certificate at a FedEx Office. I laminated it. The lamination is archival. The certificate is archival. The art is archival. Everything is archival except the frame, which is from IKEA.

Curatorial Notes

Artie Fact's work collapses the distinction between artwork and documentation — the certificate IS the artwork, the provenance IS the piece. The $40 IKEA frame is not part of the work. The $15 notary fee is not part of the work. The $890,000 price is very much part of the work.
