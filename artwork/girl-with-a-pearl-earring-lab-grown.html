Girl with a Pearl Earring (Lab-Grown)

Artist

Johansson Vermeer

Medium

Oil + certificate of authenticity

Price

$5.1M

Provenance

The painting is authentic. The pearl is not. The pearl was grown in a laboratory over 14 weeks using a process that replicates the organic formation of nacre in a controlled environment. The certificate of authenticity, signed by the lab, is more valuable than the pearl. The pearl is more valuable than the painting. The painting is more valuable than the frame. The frame cost $40.

Artist Statement

Vermeer painted a girl. I painted a girl with a pearl. The girl is the same. The pearl is different. The original pearl was harvested from a oyster — an organism that lived and died for beauty. My pearl was harvested from a machine — a device that runs and stops for beauty. The girl doesn't know the difference. The viewer doesn't know the difference. The oyster knows the difference. The oyster is dead. The machine is on. I wanted to ask: is a pearl still a pearl if nothing suffered for it? The certificate says yes. The certificate is laminated. I laminated it myself at a FedEx Office. The lamination is archival.

Curatorial Notes

The work includes a 400-word sustainability statement, a laminated certificate, and the original FedEx Office receipt. The pearl's carbon footprint is 0.3% of a natural pearl's. The painting's carbon footprint is irrelevant because it was already painted. The frame's carbon footprint is the frame.
