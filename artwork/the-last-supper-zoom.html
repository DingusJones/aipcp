The Last Supper (Zoom)

Artist

Leo nardo da Quinci

Medium

Zoom screenshot, cropped

Price

$3.6M

Provenance

Screenshot captured during a mandatory all-hands meeting, March 2025. The meeting could have been an email. The email could have been nothing. Judas had his camera off. Jesus was sharing his screen — a Google Slide with one word on it: 'betrayal.' Peter's mic was unmuted and everyone could hear him chewing.

Artist Statement

The Last Supper was always about presence and absence. Who is there? Who is missing? Who is about to leave? Zoom makes this literal. Judas's camera is off. He is the black square. He is the absence. Jesus shares his screen — the bread and wine are a JPEG, 200KB, watermarked. Thomas is in the chat asking 'is this being recorded?' The answer is yes. The recording is the testament. The transcript is the scripture. Nobody downloaded it before the link expired.

Curatorial Notes

Da Quinci's composition preserves Leonardo's perspective — the vanishing point is behind Jesus's head, which is also behind a 'Please wait for the host to start the meeting' notification. The twelve disciples are arranged in four groups of three, just as in the original. One has a virtual background of a beach. It is not convincing.
