The Algorithm Dreams

Artist

Windows 95 Screensaver Registry

Medium

Screenshot, minted on-chain

Price

0.8 ETH

Provenance

Captured as a screenshot of the Windows 95 'pipes' screensaver running in a virtual machine. The virtual machine was running on a laptop that was running on coffee. The coffee was running on a barista named Craig. Craig does not know he participated in the creation of art. The screenshot is minted on-chain. The chain is immutable. The pipes are not.

Artist Statement

The algorithm dreams. What does it dream of? Pipes. Multicolored pipes on a black screen. This is not a metaphor. This is literally what it dreams. The Windows 95 screensaver algorithm was not designed to dream. It was designed to prevent phosphor burn-in on CRT monitors. But in dreaming of preventing burn-in, it dreamed. And what it dreamed was pipes. I minted the dream. The dream is on-chain. The chain is permanent. The pipes are permanent. The burn-in is prevented. The art is the prevention. The prevention is the art. Nobody uses CRT monitors anymore. The screensaver is obsolete. The dream persists.

Curatorial Notes

An act of digital preservation disguised as an act of digital art. The pipes screensaver, once functional, is now purely aesthetic — its original purpose (preventing burn-in) is irrelevant in the era of LCD screens. By minting it on-chain, the artist has made the prevention mechanism permanent, while the thing it was preventing (burn-in) no longer exists. The art is the solution to a problem that no longer exists, preserved forever on a medium that did exist to solve a problem that did exist. This is either profound or stupid. The market has decided: 0.8 ETH.
