Starry Night (Unoptimized)

Artist

Vince van Go

Medium

16-color GIF, reduced for faster loading

Price

$1.9M

Provenance

Originally a 4-megabyte PNG. Compressed to 14KB for faster loading on a website nobody visits. The compression is lossy. The loss is permanent. The stars are fewer now. Acquired by AIPCP from a GeoCities archive that was scheduled for deletion.

Artist Statement

Van Gogh painted the sky as he saw it — swirling, alive, infinite. I painted it as the algorithm sees it — banded, dithered, 16 colors. The GIF format imposes constraints. The constraints impose beauty. Or maybe they impose ugliness. The difference, at 14KB, is academic. I wanted to ask: what survives compression? The stars? The swirl? The feeling of looking up and feeling small? The answer is: no. None of it survives. What survives is the file. The file is 14KB. The emotional weight is immeasurable. These two facts coexist without resolving.

Curatorial Notes

A meditation on lossy compression as a metaphor for memory. The night sky, once infinite, is now 16 colors. The village below is 4 colors. The cypress tree is 2. Van Go's palette reduction is not an aesthetic choice but a file-size mandate. The beauty that remains is the beauty that fit.
