Collections / Sub-Standard Submissions / Feline Energy #3

Feline Energy #3

Artist

@nft_hustler_xv

Medium

Blurry cat photo, upscaled

Price

2.1 ETH

Provenance

Part of a 47-piece collection of blurry cat photos. The cat is named Mr. Whiskers. Mr. Whiskers has not consented to being photographed, minted, or sold. Mr. Whiskers was asleep on a couch. The photo was taken at 1/4 second shutter speed by accident. The blur is unintentional. The NFT is intentional. The cat is unaware.

Artist Statement

i took a pic of my cat lol. its blurry because he moved. i minted it because someone said i could sell it. i dont know what ethereum is. my cousin told me to do this. the cat's name is mr whiskers. he's chillin. the photo is worth 2.1 ETH because its part of a 'collection' and collections are worth more than single photos apparently. i have 46 more blurry cat photos. they are all mr whiskers. he sleeps in different positions. the positions are the variation. the blur is the aesthetic. i dont know what aesthetic means but my cousin said to say that.

Curatorial Notes

A pivotal work in the 'accidental photography as intentional art' movement, Feline Energy #3 raises questions about consent — not of the photographer, but of Mr. Whiskers, who has not been compensated and does not know he is an NFT. AIPCP has reached out to Mr. Whiskers for comment. Mr. Whiskers was asleep.
