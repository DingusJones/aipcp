Mona Lisa (After the Filter)

Artist

Leanardo da Vinci Jr.

Medium

Digital, FaceTuned, 47 layers

Price

$4.2M

Provenance

Attributed to Leanardo da Vinci Jr. (b. 1997, Trenton). Executed during a period of deep introspection and moderate Instagram usage. Previously held in the collection of a guy named Kyle who listed it on Facebook Marketplace for $200. Acquired by AIPCP in 2025 for $4.2M. Kyle is not aware of this transaction.

Artist Statement

I wanted to ask: what happens when the most famous face in history gets curated for the feed? The smile hasn't changed. The smoothing has. She's had work done. Not surgical — algorithmic. The filter is the medium. The medium is the message. The message is: your fave looks tired. I painted this in Procreate over three weeks, mostly at 2 AM, mostly while avoiding other responsibilities. The 47 layers each represent a layer of self-doubt. The final layer is just 'exposure +12.' I think that says everything.

Curatorial Notes

The work interrogates the intersection of Renaissance idealism and late-stage algorithmic beauty standards. Also, her eyes are now symmetrical and deeply unsettling. This is intentional. Probably.
