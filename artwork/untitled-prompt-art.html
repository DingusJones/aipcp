Untitled (Prompt: "Art")

Artist

Prompt McSingleword

Medium

AI-generated, single prompt

Price

2.1 ETH

Provenance

Generated by typing the word 'art' into a popular AI image generator. The generation took 3.7 seconds. The artist statement took 4 days. The statement is longer than the creative process. This is noted without irony. Acquired by AIPCP from the artist, who has since forgotten the password to their wallet.

Artist Statement

I wrote the word 'art.' I pressed enter. The machine made art. The art exists. I did not make it. I made the prompt. The prompt was one word. The word was 'art.' Is the prompt the art? Is the output the art? Is the space between the prompt and the output the art? I have written 2,000 words about this. The 2,000 words are not the art. The 2,000 words are about the art. The art is one word and 3.7 seconds of computation. Everything else is commentary. I am the commentary. I am not the artist. I am the person who typed one word. The machine is the artist. I am the prompt. The prompt is: art. This statement has been peer-reviewed by no one. The peers were busy generating their own art. Their prompts were also one word.

Curatorial Notes

The radical minimalism of inquiry — McSingleword's practice reduces artistic labor to a single semantic gesture. The output, a grayish-brownish composition of no particular distinction, raises the question: does the artist's intent matter when the intent is one word long? The 2,000-word statement suggests the artist's real medium is not the prompt but the justification of the prompt. The justification is longer than the creative process. The justification is the art. The art is the justification. This circular reasoning is itself a commentary on the circular reasoning of the art market.
