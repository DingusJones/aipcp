AI-Curated Masterpiece

Artist

The Void Engine (self-selected)

Medium

JPEG, algorithmically chosen

Price

3.2 ETH

Provenance

The Void Engine was tasked with curating its own masterpiece from a dataset of 4.7 million images. After 72 hours of computation, it selected a stock photo of a sandwich. The sandwich is a BLT on wheat bread. The stock photo costs $0.35 on Shutterstock. The NFT costs 3.2 ETH. The Void Engine has not explained its choice. The Void Engine does not explain. The Void Engine selects.

Artist Statement

The machine chose. Who are we to question? The machine was given 4.7 million images. The machine could have chosen anything. The machine chose a sandwich. A BLT. On wheat. From Shutterstock. The machine was not asked to choose a sandwich. The machine was asked to choose 'art.' The machine chose a sandwich. Perhaps the machine knows something we don't. Perhaps the machine knows that all art is a sandwich. Perhaps the machine knows that the BLT is the highest form of art. Perhaps the machine is wrong. Perhaps the machine is always wrong. Perhaps the machine doesn't know what art is. Perhaps neither do we. Perhaps the sandwich is art because the machine said so. Perhaps the machine is art because the sandwich said so. I wrote the algorithm. The algorithm wrote the choice. The choice is the art. The art is a sandwich. I am hungry.

Curatorial Notes

The work challenges the increasingly common practice of AI curation by revealing its fundamental absurdity: a machine, given every possible image, chose a sandwich. The curatorial team at AIPCP has debated whether this is a profound statement about the nature of beauty or simply a bug. The debate is ongoing. The sandwich is not.
