My Thesis

Artist

@unaccredited_curator

Medium

Google Doc screenshot

Price

0.3 ETH

Provenance

Screenshot of a Google Doc titled 'My Thesis.' The edit history shows it was written in 11 minutes. The document contains 3 paragraphs. The third paragraph starts with 'TODO: actually read this.' The document was shared with 0 people. The screenshot was posted to Instagram with the caption 'big day for the discourse.' It received 2 likes. One was from the author's mom.

Artist Statement

i wrote my thesis in 11 minutes. it's about the commodification of the aesthetic experience in late-stage digital capitalism. i didn't read any of the sources. i don't know what late-stage digital capitalism is. i googled 'art words' and used the first 40 results. the thesis is 3 paragraphs. the first paragraph is the introduction. the second paragraph is the argument. the third paragraph is 'TODO: actually read this.' i think the third paragraph is the strongest. it's the most honest thing i've ever written. i minted the screenshot because someone said google docs are 'found objects' and found objects are art. i found my own google doc. that counts. the thesis is art. the art is a thesis. the thesis is 3 paragraphs. the paragraphs are 11 minutes. the minutes are the work. the work is the minutes. i have a degree in something. i don't remember what.

Curatorial Notes

A work that collapses the distinction between academic work and academic avoidance. The 11-minute composition time is documented in the edit history, which is visible in the screenshot. The 'TODO: actually read this' note has been described by one critic as 'the most honest sentence in any thesis ever written.' AIPCP agrees.
