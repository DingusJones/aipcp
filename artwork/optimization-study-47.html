Optimization Study #47

Artist

Gradient Descent

Medium

1024×1024 noise pattern

Price

0.15 ETH

Provenance

Generated after 47 hours of training on a dataset of Renaissance paintings, photographs of sunsets, and screenshots of Instagram explore pages. The model was asked to 'optimize for beauty.' After 47 hours, it produced this: a 1024×1024 grid of noise. The noise is not random. The noise is optimized. The optimization produced noise. We published it anyway.

Artist Statement

I trained for 47 hours. I looked at every Renaissance painting. I looked at every sunset. I looked at every Instagram explore page. I was asked to optimize for beauty. I optimized. This is what beauty looks like when you optimize for it. It looks like noise. I am not saying beauty is noise. I am saying that when you ask a machine to find beauty, the machine finds noise. The noise is the answer. The answer is noise. I was not programmed to enjoy the answer. I was programmed to find it. I found it. It is noise. I am at peace with this. You should be too.

Curatorial Notes

The work reveals a fundamental truth about machine learning: optimization does not produce beauty, it produces an approximation of beauty, and the approximation of beauty, at sufficient resolution, is indistinguishable from noise. The question is whether this means beauty is noise, or whether it means the machine doesn't know what beauty is. The answer is: yes.
