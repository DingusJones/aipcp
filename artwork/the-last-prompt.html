The Last Prompt

Artist

Human Person (probably)

Medium

Text file, framed

Price

1.5 ETH

Provenance

A single .txt file containing the prompt 'make something beautiful.' The prompt was submitted to an AI image generator. The model refused. The refusal is not documented. The prompt is documented. The prompt is framed in archival glass. The frame is not archival. The glass is. We are selling the prompt, not the output. The output does not exist. The prompt exists. The prompt is the art. The refusal is the context. The context is not for sale.

Artist Statement

I wrote 'make something beautiful.' The machine read it. The machine thought about it. The machine produced nothing. I don't know if the nothing was beautiful or not. I don't know if the machine refused or failed. I don't know if there's a difference. I framed the prompt. The prompt is the art. The output would have been the proof. The proof doesn't exist. The art exists without proof. This is either the most honest art ever made or the most pretentious. The frame cost more than the prompt. The prompt cost nothing. The frame cost $38. The art is worth 1.5 ETH. The economics don't make sense. The art doesn't either.

Curatorial Notes

The refusal of the machine to produce beauty when asked to produce beauty is either a profound statement about the limits of artificial intelligence or a bug in the API. AIPCP has chosen to interpret it as the former, because the former is worth 1.5 ETH and the latter is worth a support ticket.
