The Creation of Adam (Outsourced)

Artist

Michelangelo Consulting LLC

Medium

Fresco, offshore

Price

$7.0M

Provenance

Commissioned via Fiverr for $47. Delivered 14 weeks late. The freelancer, based in a timezone 14 hours ahead, communicated exclusively through automated messages. The final file was 72 DPI. AIPCP acquired it for $7M. The freelancer has not been informed of this valuation. The Fiverr review reads: 'good work, would commission humanity again.'

Artist Statement

God reaches toward Adam. Adam reaches toward God. Between them: a 14-hour time zone difference. Their fingers never touch. This is not a theological statement. This is a scheduling conflict. I outsourced the creation of man to a freelancer I found on Fiverr. The freelancer was talented. The freelancer was affordable. The freelancer was in a different timezone. We never overlapped. The brief said 'fingers almost touching, divine spark, the moment of creation.' The freelancer delivered 'two hands reaching, not touching, 72 DPI.' I think the freelancer understood the assignment better than I did. The gap between the fingers is the work. The DPI is a bonus.

Curatorial Notes

Michelangelo Consulting LLC is a collective of one freelancer who also designs logos. The commission was completed using a tablet and Procreate, then transferred to a fresco substrate via a process the freelancer described as 'I will send you the file, you figure out the wall.' The wall was figured out. The file was 72 DPI. Enlarged, the fingers are pixelated. The divine spark is a JPEG artifact.
