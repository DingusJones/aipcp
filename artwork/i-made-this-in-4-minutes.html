I Made This in 4 Minutes

Artist

@ai_bro_2024

Medium

AI-generated image, timer screenshot

Price

0.8 ETH

Provenance

An AI-generated image accompanied by a screenshot of a timer reading 4:00. The image was generated by typing 'cool art' into an AI image generator. The timer was started before the prompt was submitted. The timer was stopped when the image was generated. The total creative process: 4 minutes. The artist statement: 6 hours. The ratio of statement to creation: 90:1. This is noted without comment.

Artist Statement

i made this in 4 minutes. the timer proves it. 4 minutes. that's how long art takes now. i typed 'cool art' and waited 4 minutes and the machine made cool art. is it cool? i don't know. the prompt said cool. the machine agreed. the timer is the performance art. the image is the evidence. the evidence is cool art. i spent 6 hours writing this statement because the statement has to be longer than the art or people don't take you seriously. the art took 4 minutes. the statement took 6 hours. the ratio is 90 to 1. this means the statement is 90 times more important than the art. or the art is 90 times more efficient than the statement. i don't know which. i do know the timer is the real art. the image is just the receipt. the receipt is cool art. the timer is cool art. i am cool art. everything is cool art if you type it.

Curatorial Notes

The work's central innovation is the inclusion of the timer as part of the piece — transforming the 4-minute generation time from an embarrassment into a feature. @ai_bro_2024 has discovered that in the age of AI generation, the process (4 minutes) is more interesting than the output (a grayish-bluish gradient of no particular distinction). The timer is the art. The art is the timer. The image is the receipt.
