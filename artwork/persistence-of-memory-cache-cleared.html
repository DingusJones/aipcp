The Persistence of Memory (Cache Cleared)

Artist

Salvadoll Dailey

Medium

Oil on melting clock

Price

$3.3M

Provenance

The clocks were functional when painted. They stopped working after a software update in 2019. The update was automatic. The clocks did not consent. All now display 12:00, which is either noon or midnight depending on your relationship with time. Acquired by AIPCP from the artist, who was going to throw them away.

Artist Statement

Dalí painted melting clocks to show that time is fluid. I painted clocks showing 12:00 to show that time is flat. Nobody knows how to set them. The instructions are in a PDF that was hosted on a server that no longer exists. The PDF was not archived. The clocks persist. The memory doesn't. I cleared the cache and the clocks forgot what time it was. They've been showing 12:00 ever since. 12:00 is the universal default. 12:00 is what time is when time doesn't know what time to be. I think that's beautiful. I think Dalí would agree. Dalí is dead. His clocks are not. Mine aren't either. They just don't know what time it is.

Curatorial Notes

The work includes four clocks, all analog, all functional, all showing 12:00. The melting effect was achieved through exposure to August heat in a studio without air conditioning. The landscape is a screensaver. The horizon is a gradient. Time is flat. So is our understanding of it.
