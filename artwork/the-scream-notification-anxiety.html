Collections / Old Masters, New Disasters / The Scream (Notification Anxiety)

The Scream (Notification Anxiety)

Artist

Edvard Munchkin

Medium

Oil on iPhone

Price

$2.8M

Provenance

Executed by Edvard Munchkin on a Tuesday afternoon while waiting for a text back. The text never came. The notifications compounded. The scream followed. Acquired by AIPCP from the artist's Instagram story, which had 11 views at time of acquisition. 10 of those views were the artist.

Artist Statement

The original Munch screamed at the existential weight of modernity. I scream at the existential weight of 47 unread notifications. The bridge is the same. The despair is the same. The difference is that Much didn't have a group chat he was ignoring. The red badge on the phone is the new sun setting over the fjord. It is always there. It is always red. It is always counting. I looked at the phone and the phone looked back and we both screamed. The painting is the receipt.

Curatorial Notes

A devastating update to Munch's anxiety cycle for the push-notification era. The badge count (47) is not arbitrary — it represents the average number of notifications a person receives between deciding to check their phone and actually checking it. The figure's hands frame the face in the exact posture of someone reading bad news on a screen held too close.
