Late Capitalism (Oil on Canvas)

Artist

@hustle_over_heals

Medium

Excel screenshot

Price

0.9 ETH

Provenance

Screenshot of the artist's actual personal finance spreadsheet. The numbers are real. The debt is real. The categories include 'rent,' 'food,' 'art supplies (never used),' 'crypto (lost),' and 'therapist.' The screenshot was taken at 11:47 PM on a Sunday. The artist was crying. The crying is not in the screenshot. The screenshot is the receipt.

Artist Statement

i screenshotted my spreadsheet. its my actual finances. i owe $47,000 in student loans. i spent $340 on art supplies i never opened. i put $200 in crypto and it's worth $12 now. my therapist costs $180 per session. i see her weekly. the spreadsheet is my most honest artwork because it's the only one that's true. every number is real. every number is me. i called it 'late capitalism' because someone on twitter said that's what everything is called now. i don't know what late capitalism means. i know what $47,000 means. it means i screenshot my debt and called it art and someone bought it for 0.9 ETH. the ETH is worth less now than when they bought it. we are both losing. the spreadsheet is still true. the debt is still there. the art is the debt. the debt is the art.

Curatorial Notes

The most emotionally honest work in the AIPCP collection. Painted in Excel. Suffered in real life. The spreadsheet's formulas still work — the debt recalculates in real time. The art appreciates. The debt does not.
