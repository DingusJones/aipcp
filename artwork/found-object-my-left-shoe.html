Found Object (My Left Shoe)

Artist

@dada_kid_99

Medium

Shoe, single, worn

Price

1.2 ETH

Provenance

One left shoe. New Balance, size 10.5. Grey. Worn during the creation of 14 other pieces in the AIPCP collection. The right shoe is not part of the work. The right shoe is in the artist's closet. The right shoe is not for sale. The left shoe is for sale. The left shoe has been worn more than the right shoe because the artist favors their left foot. Provenance: smell confirms authenticity.

Artist Statement

this is my left shoe. i wore it while making 14 other artworks. the shoe is part of the creative process. without the shoe i would have been barefoot. barefoot art is different from shoed art. the shoe influenced the art. the art influenced the shoe. the shoe is now art. the art is now shoe. i'm selling the left shoe because the left shoe did more work. the right shoe was along for the ride. the right shoe is not for sale because the right shoe is lazy. if you buy the left shoe you are buying 14 artworks worth of creative energy. the creative energy is stored in the sole. the sole is worn. the wearing is the art. the smell is the provenance. if it doesn't smell it's not mine. it smells. it's mine. it's art. it's $1.2 ETH. i will need a new left shoe. i will wear the new left shoe for the next 14 artworks. the cycle continues. the shoe cycle. the art cycle. they are the same cycle. i walk in circles. the circles are art.

Curatorial Notes

@dada_kid_99 continues the Duchampian tradition of the readymade, with one key innovation: the readymade has been worn. The shoe's provenance is confirmed by smell — a first in the art world, where provenance is typically confirmed by documentation. The documentation here is olfactory. The right shoe remains in the artist's closet. It is not art. It is a shoe. The distinction is the work.
