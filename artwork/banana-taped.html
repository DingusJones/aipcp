Collections / Priced to Groan / Banana (Taped)

Banana (Taped)

Artist

Maurizio Catt-elán't

Medium

Banana, duct tape, wall

Price

$6.2M

Provenance

Purchased from a guy named Steve in a Whole Foods parking lot on Houston Street. Price: $0.35. Steve was eating a second banana at the time and did not understand the significance of the transaction. Steve has not been contacted since. The banana has been taped to the wall of the AIPCP gallery (a WeWork that closed) since July 2025. The banana has browned. The browning is part of the work. The tape is not part of the work. The tape is just tape. The wall is not part of the work. The wall is a wall. The valuation is $6.2M. Steve's cut was $0.35.

Artist Statement

I did not make this. I found this. I taped it. The taping is the art. The banana is the material. The wall is the context. The price is the joke. The joke is the price. I have nothing else to say. The banana says it all. The banana is browning. The brown is the passage of time. The tape is the attempt to hold it in place. The wall is the institution. The institution is the frame. The frame is the market. The market is the banana. We are all the banana. I am the tape.

Curatorial Notes

A meditation on value, impermanence, and the art market's willingness to pay $6.2M for produce. The banana has not been replaced since installation. It is now brown. It is now soft. It is now, in the words of one visitor, 'definitely not a banana anymore.' The tape holds. The banana does not. This is the work.
