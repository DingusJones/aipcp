American Still Life

Artist

@mclovin_official

Medium

McDonald's app screenshot

Price

0.5 ETH

Provenance

Screenshot of the McDonald's mobile app. The cart contains one large fry, one McChicken, and a medium Coke. The total is $8.47. The screenshot was captured at 1:23 AM. The artist described this as 'a moment of vulnerability.' The order was placed. The order was consumed. The screenshot remains. The art is the screenshot. The screenshot is the meal. The meal is gone.

Artist Statement

it was 1am and i was hungry. i opened the mcdonalds app. i added a large fry. i added a mcchicken. i added a medium coke. the total was $8.47. i screenshotted it because the colors looked nice. then i realized the screenshot was my most american artwork. a still life. a 21st century still life. the dutch painted fruit and skulls. i screenshot fries and a sandwich. the fries are the fruit. the mcchicken is the skull. the coke is the wine. the app is the canvas. the screenshot is the brush. i am the painter. i am the consumer. i am the painting. the painting costs $8.47 to recreate. it costs 0.5 ETH to own. the difference is the art. the art is the difference. i ate the meal. the meal was good. the art is better. the art is a screenshot. the screenshot is forever. the meal was not.

Curatorial Notes

McLovin Official's American Still Life updates the Dutch still life tradition for the app economy: where 17th-century painters depicted lemons, grapes, and hourglasses to evoke the transience of worldly pleasures, McLovin depicts a large fry, a McChicken, and a medium Coke to evoke the transience of a 1 AM decision. The total ($8.47) is the modern memento mori.
