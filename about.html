About the Institute

Previously known as nothing.

"The Art-ifice Institute exists to interrogate, destabilize, and recontextualize the ontological frameworks through which aesthetic value is constructed, commodified, and Instagrammed. We are not a museum. We are not a gallery. We are a praxis. Whatever that means."
— Mission Statement, approved by the board, unread by the public

History

Founded in 2024 by Ivana Curratored with a $400M capital campaign. $399.2M went to administrative costs. $800K went to art. The building does not exist. The institute operates out of a WeWork that closed. The WeWork's address is still on the letterhead. The letterhead is a Google Doc. The Google Doc is shared with 3 people, 2 of whom are the same person.

The institute was conceived as a response to the question: "What if art, but money?" The answer, 18 months later, is this website. The website cost $47 to host. The art on it is valued at $94M. The ratio of value to cost is the praxis. The praxis is the ratio.
