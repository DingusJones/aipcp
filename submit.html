Open Call: Submissions

The Art-ifice Institute accepts submissions from anyone, regardless of talent, training, or good judgment.

Submission Guidelines

  1. All mediums accepted. Including medium-rare.
  2. Submissions must be original. Or original enough. We're not your art history professor.
  3. Please include a 200-word artist statement. We will not read it.
  4. JPEG, PNG, TIFF, BMP, GIF, or screenshot of a file that is one of those.
  5. File size limit: none. Emotional size limit: also none.
  6. Submissions reviewed by Otto Matic, our AI programs director. Otto has fired the HR director and cannot be stopped.
